# Public enums that may stay exhaustive even though they are new since v1.0.0.
#
# One per line: <rustdoc path> <one-line reason>. The path is the definition
# path exactly as check_new_public_enums.py reports it (crate name first,
# through the module the enum is defined in, not through any re-export).
# Blank lines and lines starting with '#' are ignored. A line without a
# reason fails the gate rather than silently allowing the enum: an entry
# here means someone decided the enum is closed for good, and that
# decision names itself.
#
# Empty by default: no exhaustive enum is allowed until someone writes
# down why it can never grow a variant (e.g. a two-state switch).
