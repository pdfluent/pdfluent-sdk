# Operations

The plant ran to schedule through the quarter and the two lines commissioned in March reached their rated output in the first week of May.

## Maintenance

The shutdown planned for August was brought forward to July so that it would fall in the same week as the inspection already booked for the boiler house, and the two were carried out together over five days.
